### The risk, found in your own workbooks

A recent forensic audit of your two controlled QC formats (ST/QC/02 · ST/QC/04) surfaced three findings that could fail a FLENDER / WINERGY audit today:

1. **Painting recorded on steel colder than its dew point** — a typed dew point of 29.2 °C against steel at 28.8 °C. Under ISO 12944-7 this is an automatic batch rejection. The real dew point was 24.1 °C; one transcription typo put it on a permanent quality record.
2. **Impossible tolerances in a live ISO 9001 template** — Nominal 10 with Min 1 / Max 3, from a broken hand-typed formula, undetected.
3. **Zero instrument traceability** — the Equipment ID column is blank on all 53 rows. Under ISO 9001, one expired gauge can invalidate every report it touched.

These are spreadsheet-system failures, not people failures. The fix must be structural.

---

### The platform

A web application that keeps your audited formats exactly — same layouts, format numbers, sign-off blocks — while making these errors impossible:

- **Dew point is computed, never typed** — painting locks out if steel is within 3 °C of dew point (ISO 12944-7)
- **Limits are derived automatically** — `(2065)`, `Ø12` read correctly; arithmetic errors become impossible
- **26-point DFT statistics live** — Min/Max/Average + ISO 19840 compliance, no hidden cells
- **Every reading linked to a calibrated instrument** — 15-day expiry alerts, 1-click audit recall
- **Two-person digital sign-off** — immutable timestamps; approved records cannot be altered by anyone
- **One-click PDF reports** — pixel-matched to your controlled formats, audit-ready footers
- **Works offline** — nothing is lost to Wi-Fi drops, crashes, or reboots
- **1-click NCR drafting** — a failed reading auto-writes 80% of the non-conformance report
